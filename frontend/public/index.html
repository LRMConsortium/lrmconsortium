    How it works

    If you are looking for a home

    1. 1

      Register and verify. One set of documents, checked once, reused for every application.

    2. 2

      Apply to a listing. The landlord sees a verified applicant, so a decision takes days rather than weeks.

    3. 3

      Pay rent through LRMC. Every payment receipted, and a record you can produce years later.

    If you own property

    1. 1

      List it. A coordinator is assigned to your property and is the person you call.

    2. 2

      We collect and chase. Rent, arrears and maintenance handled without you being the one to ring anybody.

    3. 3

      Get paid on a schedule. Payouts you can forecast, with the arithmetic shown.
